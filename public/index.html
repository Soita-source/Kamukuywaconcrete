About Us

Building Kenya's Future, One Load at a Time

Kamukuywa Concrete Limited is a leading supplier of construction materials in Western Kenya. We specialize in providing high-quality river sand, ballast, concrete poles, and building stones.

Our mission is to simplify construction for builders and homeowners by ensuring timely delivery and fair pricing. Whether you are building a home or a commercial complex, we have the materials you need.

  • Reliable fleet for fast delivery
  • Committed to quality standards
